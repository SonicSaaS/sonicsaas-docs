# Microsoft 365

SonicSaaS connects to your Microsoft 365 tenant and reports on how it is configured — where it is
strong, where it is exposed, and what to fix first.

Everything here is **read-only**. SonicSaaS looks at settings and reports on them. It does not change
your tenant, move your data, or read the contents of your files and email.

## Copilot Readiness

Microsoft 365 Copilot is an AI assistant that works across your documents, email, chats and meetings.
The single most important thing to understand about it is this:

> **Copilot inherits each user's existing access.** It does not have permissions of its own. If a
> person can already open a file, Copilot can read that file and summarise it back to them. If your
> files are shared more widely than you realise, Copilot is very good at finding them.

That makes "are we ready for Copilot?" a question about how your tenant is set up, not a switch you
flip. **Copilot Readiness** grades a tenant from A to F across seven areas and shows you exactly which
checks are passing, which are failing, and what to do about each one.

- **[Copilot Readiness](/m365/copilot-readiness)** — how the grade works, and the two scores you'll see

Each area has its own page, explaining every individual check:

- **[Licensing](/m365/copilot-licensing)** — the subscriptions Copilot needs before it can be turned on
- **[Identity & Access](/m365/copilot-identity)** — how well sign-ins are protected
- **[Sensitivity Labels](/m365/copilot-sensitivity-labels)** — marking sensitive documents so Copilot respects them
- **[Data Loss Prevention](/m365/copilot-dlp)** — rules that stop sensitive information leaving
- **[Access Governance](/m365/copilot-access-governance)** — how widely files are shared
- **[Audit & Monitoring](/m365/copilot-audit)** — whether Copilot activity is recorded
